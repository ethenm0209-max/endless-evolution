📘 Help: Controls, Forge & Settings

Combat: WASD move, mouse attack, Space dash, Q Chi, V abilities, M shop, B bestiary, H potion. Equip unlocked moves in Abilities; select a hotbar slot and use E. Hold supported Gum-Gum attacks to charge.

Forge: Collect materials from enemies, open the Forge, select a recipe, place its ingredients into the 3×3 grid, then craft. Your inventory shows what you own.

Settings: Select graphics quality, adjust audio, change PC keybinds then Confirm & Save. On mobile, use Edit HUD to drag controls, or Reset if controls move off-screen.

Multiplayer: Enter your room-server address, create a private code, or join your friend's code. The host can kick players and start the room.

📜 Help: Quests, Scrolls & Items

Open Abilities → Evolution Tree, choose an unlocked power, then accept its quest. Quests ask you to use that power, defeat enemies and gather materials. Each power earns its own named scroll points. Harder powers need rarer materials. Use the Item & Scroll Quest Guide in Abilities to find enemy drops. Open Tasks next to Shop to see pinned quests, completed quests and claim rewards.

v7.5 · Meteor Damage & Fire

• Meteor damage now lands when the meteor touches the ground, not before.
• Impact leaves a burning fire circle for 1.5 seconds.
• Enemies in the flames flash orange-red and take 5 extra burn damage over the effect duration.

v7.4 · Gum-Gum Charged Attacks

• Hold Shift+Z or Shift+X for 1.35 seconds to fully charge Rifle or Elephant Pistol; short presses remain unchanged.
